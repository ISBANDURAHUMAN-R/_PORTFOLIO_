A short introduction to what I care about and how I work.

I'm an Artificial Intelligence & Data Science student at Panimalar Engineering College, drawn to the space where software engineering, machine learning and good design overlap. Most of what I know, I learned by building — taking an idea, wiring it together, and fixing what broke along the way.

I spend my time across backend development, data science and interface design, and I like testing that knowledge in hackathons and competitions rather than keeping it theoretical. When something interests me — a new library, a model architecture, an interaction pattern — I'd rather build a small version of it than just read about it.
